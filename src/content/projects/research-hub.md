## Overview

Research Hub is a research facility and instrument management and booking platform for university staff and researchers.

## Purpose and scope

The platform brings facility and instrument listings, availability, booking requests and approvals into a research-management application.

## My role

Independently planned and developed end-to-end.

## Features

- Facility and instrument listings, availability and booking
- Booking requests and approvals
- Researcher and staff accounts
- Inventory and equipment management
- Razorpay payments
- Document and image uploads
- Email notifications
- Administrative dashboards and reports

## Technology

The project stack includes Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma and Tailwind CSS 4. Interface tooling includes shadcn/ui, Radix UI and TanStack Table.

Other components include JWT, bcryptjs, MinIO S3-compatible storage, Razorpay and Nodemailer.

## Testing tools

Vitest and React Testing Library are included in the project tooling. Test coverage and detailed test results are not published here.
