## Overview

WaterBuddy is a desktop companion that moves around the desktop, provides hydration reminders and offers playful interactions.

## Purpose and scope

The project combines a desktop character with water reminders, animation states, reminder settings and interaction features.

## My role

Independently developed.

## Features

- Desktop walking and hydration reminders
- Drinking, sitting, sleeping and idle animations
- Character Studio
- Reminder settings and system tray
- Click interactions and games

## Technology

The project uses Python, PySide6 and Qt, with Qt timers, PNG animation frames and QPainter. JSON is used for settings and manifests; the tooling also includes Pillow and NumPy.

## Testing and packaging tools

The project tooling includes Python unittest and PyInstaller. Test results, packaging artifacts and public downloads are not published here yet.
