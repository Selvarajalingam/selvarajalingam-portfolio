# Creative Direction

## Overall feeling
Premium, calm, confident, creative and human. A carefully art-directed digital studio, not a résumé template.

Avoid generic developer layouts, excessive neon, glass cards everywhere, decorative fake terminals, fake dashboards, unlicensed stock, excessive particles, and 3D on every page.

## Hero
Deep black canvas, atmospheric violet light, original tactile metallic sculptural form. Headline oversized and editorial with selective violet emphasis. Identity line, concise supporting copy, two CTAs. Sculpture is primary; portrait is secondary and temporary/fictional until user's real photo is supplied. Small project-preview fragments must not compete.

## Intro
Stroke-drawn `selva.` resolves to wordmark, sculpture emerges, then hero reveals. First visit only, skippable, replayable, reduced-motion aware, never blocks content.

## Homepage art direction
1 Hero: cinematic, generous negative space.
2 Introduction: calm editorial text.
3 Featured Work: asymmetric project grid.
4 Services: three concise pillars.
5 Journey: timeline.
6 Impact: typographic statements, verified figures only.
7 Skills: interactive categorized ecosystem.
8 AWS/Cloud: resource tiles only when real content exists.
9 Ventures: four brand-aware product tiles.
10 Lab: compact teaser with honest empty state.
11 Connect: large closing invitation.

## Page-specific
- About: human story, education, experience, skills, compact Beyond the Code.
- Work: featured-first editorial grid; secondary numbered archive and usable filters.
- Case study: cinematic project opening, then factual story, architecture, technical detail and lessons.
- Secondary cases: compact template; don't pad limited facts.
- Lab: clear statuses, no invented experiments.
- Ventures: preserve each product's actual logo and palette within shared layout.
- Services: clean accordion.
- Experience: timeline with factual contributions.
- Contact: cinematic but practical form; usability first.
- Footer: large `selva.`, “Teach. Build. Create. Innovate. Repeat.”, contact, socials, sitemap and privacy.

## Responsive
Desktop: full editorial layout and 3D if capable.
Tablet: simplify grids and sculpture.
Mobile: content-first stack, light assets, touch-friendly controls and accessible navigation.
Reduced motion: static composition and immediate content.
